About this shop program

This is a small, appointment-only barber messaging program — not a marketing blast list. After you opt in, you text the same number to manage your bookings. Messages are transactional customer-care texts about your appointments.

Who it’s for: clients in Hayward and the wider Bay Area who want to schedule a cut by SMS, get a clear confirmation, and receive a reminder before they come in.

What you can do over SMS

Check openings Ask what’s free on a day or time that works for you.
Book Reserve a slot for the cut type you want.
Confirm Get a clear yes on the time once it’s set.
Reminders Receive a heads-up before your appointment.
Reschedule Move your booking if plans change.
Cancel Cancel over text when you can’t make it.

How SMS booking works

  1. Opt in Text START to +1 (510) 571-4894. You’ll get a short confirmation that you’re opted in.
  2. Ask for a time Reply with the day, time window, and cut type you want (for example: Saturday afternoon fade).
  3. Get booked The program checks openings and confirms your appointment by text.
  4. Stay updated You’ll get confirmation and reminder messages. Text anytime to reschedule or cancel. Reply STOP if you no longer want messages.
