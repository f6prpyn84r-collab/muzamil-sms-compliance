This Privacy Policy explains how mobile information is collected and used for SMS appointment booking and related customer-care messages sent by Muzamil Suliman.

Information we collect

When you opt in to SMS by texting START to +1 (510) 571-4894, we collect your mobile phone number and the content of messages you exchange with the program (for example requests to check availability, book, reschedule, or cancel an appointment, and HELP / STOP requests).

How we use information

We use your mobile number and message content only to provide appointment booking customer care: scheduling, confirmations, reminders, reschedule and cancellation notices, and replies to your booking questions.

Message frequency varies based on your appointments and replies. You may receive several messages related to a single appointment (for example confirmation, reminder, and schedule change). Message and data rates may apply.

Sharing of mobile information

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent / opt-in data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile data will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes at any time.

We do not sell or rent mobile phone numbers. Service providers that transmit SMS (such as our messaging carrier/platform) process messages solely to deliver this appointment booking service and not for their own marketing.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP to cancel. Reply HELP for help. You may also email sulimanmuzamil@gmail.com.
