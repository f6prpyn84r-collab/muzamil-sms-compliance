Program description

This SMS program sends customer-care messages about barber / haircut appointment booking, availability replies, confirmations, reminders, rescheduling, and cancellations for clients who have opted in. Messages are transactional appointment messages, not advertising blasts and not affiliate or third-party lead-generation marketing.

How to opt in

Text START to +1 (510) 571-4894. You will receive a confirmation SMS. By texting START, you consent to receive recurring appointment booking messages from Muzamil Suliman until you opt out.

Public Call to Action and disclosures: Opt-In / CTA page. Business home: website.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies based on your appointments and replies. You may receive several messages per booking cycle. Message and data rates may apply.

Support

For help, reply HELP or INFO to any message from this program, or email sulimanmuzamil@gmail.com.

Opt-out

You can cancel anytime by replying STOP (or STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT). After you opt out, you will receive a confirmation and no further program messages unless you opt in again by texting START.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. We do not share, sell, or provide mobile phone numbers or messaging consent / opt-in data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile data will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes at any time.

Consent to receive SMS is collected directly for this program by texting START (see the Call to Action). Consent is not bundled into unrelated agreements and is not transferred for third-party marketing.
